How animation works

Each part moves by channel + phase + amplitude:

  • channel — which movement drives it: limb (walking legs), arm (also walk-driven), wing (flap), flutter (slow sway, always on), none (still).
  • phase — where in the cycle it sits, in degrees. Legs at 0 and 180 step alternately; 0/60/120/180 makes a wave.
  • amplitude — how far it swings, in degrees. 26° is a normal walk, 5° a small tremble.

Parts with the same channel and phase move together as one piece — give a shoe its leg's settings and it rides along. Nothing is assumed about your character: a snake is limb at 0/60/120/180, a ghost is flutter everywhere.

Names come from the Inkscape layer/group ids. Hover a part to see which vector it is.
